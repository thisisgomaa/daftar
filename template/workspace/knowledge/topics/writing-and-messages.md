# Writing and messages
> Open when: Drafting emails, chats, posts, or any text that may leave the machine
> New lesson: `daftar kb add writing-and-messages "one-line lesson" --by <name>`

## Owner guidance

## Lessons
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] State inferred facts as a confirmation question ("We understood X, is that right?"), not as an assertion.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Before drafting, read the decisions already settled in the project notes; drafts written from the brief alone reopen closed points.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] When asked "how should we reply?", first discuss the request and your understanding; write the draft last.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] A copy request means the copy only: no notes, caveats, or explanations around it.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Cut AI tells from outgoing text: em dashes, "I hope this finds you well", "delve", "don't hesitate to reach out".
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] When triaging mail, separate people asking for your work from people selling theirs; an unknown sender pitching a service is noise, even under a fake "Re:".
