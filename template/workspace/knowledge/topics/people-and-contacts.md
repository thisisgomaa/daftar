## Lessons
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Speech-to-text mangles proper names; confirm names with the owner before writing them into project files.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Automatic transcripts do not separate speakers reliably; ask who said what instead of attributing from context.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Before creating a contact, search every spelling and script variant of the name to avoid duplicates.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Take phone numbers, emails, and signatures from one canonical facts file, never from old chats or lead notes.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Never guess a phone number or recipient; if the target is ambiguous, ask for an explicit one instead of retrying.
