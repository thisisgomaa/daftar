> Open when: Generating, editing, or reviewing images and media assets

## Lessons
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] When the owner sends a reference, extract real frames or crops from it first and get approval before generating anything.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Use the original asset as the edit input, never an earlier generated image; small text degrades with every generation.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] A model cannot faithfully copy small text, logos, or stamps; keep the original asset and overlay it, and offer this early instead of retrying.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] To check spelling, crop and enlarge the label next to the original in one image; reviewers miss distortions at full-frame size.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Generate scenes without text and set typography afterwards in a design tool with the real fonts.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] A product's real colors are fixed; never change the product to satisfy a styling rule.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Generate the final master at full size; upscaling or editing a small draft gives a soft, noisy result.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Chromium PDFs turn blur shadows, gradients, and opacity into soft-masked images that mobile viewers drop; use flat colors and inspect the PDF itself, not a browser screenshot.
