## Lessons
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] `rclone lsf` without `--files-only` also lists folders, and `rclone delete` on a folder removes everything inside it; prune with `--files-only` and explicit filters.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Cloud drive deletes usually go to trash and keep counting against quota for weeks; bypass or empty the trash when pruning for space.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] A folder name that differs between local and cloud creates a duplicate folder on sync; keep one explicit name mapping.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Delivery folders hold deliverables only: no internal notes, manifests, or source files.
- 2026-11-09 [daftar] Before saying an asset is missing, look inside existing PDFs: they often embed the full-resolution originals (`pdfimages -list`).
